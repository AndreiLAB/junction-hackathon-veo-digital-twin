# Dataset card: `synthetic_geo_dataset` v1 (shipped WITH a known defect)

**Read this before training.** This dataset was generated on a low-memory laptop and shipped as-is on purpose so the training machine can start.
**It has a known, systematic labelling defect (section 3). The remedy is meant to be done on the training machine (section 4) before the final training run.**
v1 is still useful: keep it as the *baseline* so v1 (noisy labels) vs v2 (fixed) can be compared honestly.

## 1. What it is
* 1759 tiles of 1280x1280 px, YOLO format. Classes: `0 abb_relion_615`, `1 other_hmi` (the black "cerdex HP" display of cabinets TSK1/TSK2, a look-alike).
* Each tile is a crop of a REAL cube-face photo (`VEO Images/img_NNN.jpg`, 4096x4096). The real relays of cabinets **H01-H05 were removed (inpainted) and replaced by
  the reference render** at the geometry-measured 3D position through the camera pose (`cameras.json`). The Cerdex displays are real and labelled in place.
* Generated by `synthetic_geo/generate_geo_synthetic.py` at repo commit `1a60d6f` (parameters and geometry: `synthetic_geo/ANTIGRAVITY_PROMPT.md`, sections 3-6).
* Split by SCAN: scans **6, 8, 13** are validation, the rest training. No flips, rotation or random sizes.

| Split | Tiles | relay boxes | other_hmi boxes | empty tiles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 1681 | 3513 | 270 | 150 |
| val | 78 | 77 | 13 | 12 |

Box width in pixels (1280 tile): train relay min 37 / median 108 / p90 265 / max 938; train other_hmi min 12 / median 75 / max 599;
**val relay min 115 / median 233** (the validation set has NO small/far relays); val other_hmi 82-554.

## 2. Files in the zip
`images/{train,val}/*.jpg`, `labels/{train,val}/*.txt` (YOLO), `data.yaml`, `meta.jsonl` (per tile: source photo, scan, tile origin, camera pose, boxes with cabinet, depth, visible fraction),
`stats.json`, this card, and four check sheets: `check_train.jpg`, `check_val.jpg`, `check_closeups.jpg`, `check_unlabeled.jpg` (evidence for defect 1).
**Not shipped:** the real photos (`VEO Images/`, separate Drive item, needed for the remedy and for the real evaluation), generation logs, and shard leftovers (`meta_2/6.jsonl` come from a crashed run: ignore).

Validated: every image has a label file; all class ids are 0/1; all coordinates inside [0,1]; 0 problems. Reviewed by eye: a 24-tile train sheet, a 12-tile val sheet and 3 full-resolution close-ups (not every tile).

## 3. KNOWN DEFECTS (v1)
1. **Unlabelled REAL ABB relays on other cabinets (systematic label noise).** Cabinet **OT1** (right of TSK1) carries **two real ABB relays** with the same keypad/LCD layout as the REX615;
   they were never replaced or labelled, so they are background in every tile that contains them. VLK and OKK1 probably also carry relays (not checked).
   Up to **760 of 1759 tiles** come from photos that show OT1/VLK/OKK1 (upper bound; not every such tile contains the relays). Effect if trained as is: the model learns "a relay on these cabinets is background".
   Measured (OT1, from img_032; the OT1 tape back-projected to within 5 mm of its registered position, so the method is sound): door plane x = -2.720 (door normal -x),
   relay A centre = OT1 position + (dy -0.188, dz +0.166) m, relay B centre = OT1 position + (dy -0.213, dz -0.152) m. Case size NOT measured (they look narrower than the H-cabinet wide case; check).
2. **Cerdex (`other_hmi`) boxes are slightly misaligned**: on TSK1 the box was seen shifted ~15-20 px in a 1280 tile (clips the left edge). Centres (TSK1 y=-5.119, TSK2 y=-4.318, z~1.56) came from eyeballed boxes.
3. **Far relays (> ~3.5 m) look faint and noisy**, with a darker smear nearby: the noise/blur matching probably over-estimates noise there.
4. **Validation has no small relays** (min 115 px vs 37 px in train), so synthetic val cannot show far-range performance. Use the real held-out set for that.
5. **Class imbalance:** `other_hmi` is ~7% of boxes; val has only 13 `other_hmi` boxes.
6. **Single site:** 28 photos show the relay cabinets; backgrounds repeat across tiles. H01's relay position came from one image (least certain).

## 4. REMEDY (do on the training machine, then regenerate the WHOLE dataset as v2; do not patch v1)
1. **Measure the relays of VLK and OKK1** (and re-measure OT1 case size and both relays). Method (as used for OT1): in a close frontal photo pick the pixel of the relay centre, back-project with
   `geometry.Camera.ray(u, v)` onto the cabinet's door plane (`x = cabinet x`), subtract the cabinet position. Confirm each by projecting the predicted box into >= 8 other photos and looking at a contact sheet.
   Do NOT use automatic template matching (it failed: NCC 0.3-0.4).
2. **Extend the geometry** (`relay_geometry.json` + `generate_geo_synthetic.py`): allow several relays per cabinet and a per-cabinet case size; add OT1/VLK/OKK1 (set `expects_relay` in `cabinet_registry.json` only after the mentors or photos confirm they are 615-series).
   Apply the same remove-and-replace and labelling as for H01-H05, including `location_gate.py`. **The cabinet list ("H01".."H05") is hard-coded in `generate_geo_synthetic.py` (`plan`, `main`) and `location_gate.py` (`Gate.expected`); extend all of them,
   otherwise the gate will reject relays on the new cabinets and `export_detections.py` will give them `position: null`.**
3. **Re-measure the Cerdex centres** from 3-4 photos and tighten the box (`relay_geometry.json: other_hmi`).
4. **Fix far-relay appearance:** cap the noise estimate (e.g. at 0.6x) and compare patch statistics against the real, unlabelled OT1 relays at similar distances (they are real far-relay references).
5. **Add small/far relays to validation** (hold out some far pairs, or rely on the real evaluation set).
6. Regenerate, run `check_dataset.py`, **look at the sheets again**, then train. Train v1 as a baseline if time allows and report v1 vs v2 on the REAL held-out photos.
7. Tell the user if OT1 (and VLK/OKK1) carry 615 relays: the tags for those cabinets would then need relay devices too (the backend currently expects relays only on H01-H05).

## 5. Rules
No images, datasets, secrets or weights > 50 MB in git. Never invent boxes or metrics. State that everything comes from one site.

## 6. v2 scope additions (decided after v1 was shipped): detect the OTHER assets, identify the UniGear cabinet
The mentors require the technique to detect other assets on the cabinet, and the UniGear panel itself must be identified. v2 therefore has four classes:
`0 abb_relion_615, 1 other_hmi (Cerdex, now a real asset), 2 unigear_zs2_panel, 3 vd4_breaker_window`. Full geometry, per-cabinet presence (VD4 window absent on H03, unmeasured on H01), the two-scale rule for the panel
(full face downscaled to 1280, not native tiles) and the identification rule are in `ANTIGRAVITY_PROMPT.md` section 5b, with the numbers in `relay_geometry.json` and `cabinet_registry.json` (`assets` per cabinet).
Panel and VD4-window labels come from geometry on REAL photos (nothing synthetic is inserted for them). Assumption: **36 kV UniGear** (the supplied manual is the 36 kV one); keep it flagged as assumed.
Also updated since v1: **H01's relay offset is dy -0.07** (was 0.0): its nameplate is 0.17 m left of the panel centre.
